XP can be increased with:

* XP Routers
* Executive Runner gamepass

Effective multiplier:

**Effective Multiplier = Router Multiplier × 2 if VIP/Executive Runner**

Final XP formula:

**XP = (Combo Score / 360,000) × Effective Multiplier**

Required combo with multiplier:

**Required Combo Score = (XP Needed × 360,000) / Effective Multiplier**
