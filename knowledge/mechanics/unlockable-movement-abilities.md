## Coil

Input:
* Descend midair

Effect:
* Raises hitbox slightly.
* Helps clear obstacles.
* Slightly increases upward reach.

Obtained:
* Level 2

## Dash

Input:
* Dash keybind

Effect:
* Increases acceleration.
* Grants bonus momentum for up to 2 seconds.
* Jumping off a ledge while dashing increases distance and speed.
* Precise landing while Dash is on cooldown instantly resets it.

Challenge:
* Dash Training

Location:
* Northeast of Riser, edge of Downtown.

## Powerslide

Input:
* Descend

Effect:
* Boosts speed momentarily.
* Can preserve chain.
* Slides under low obstacles.

Challenge:
* The Art of Powerslide

Location:
* South of Solar building.

## Dropdown

Input:
* Descend / Powerslide toward ledge

Effect:
* Powersliding toward a ledge activates Dropdown.
* Slightly reduces fall damage.
* Increases survivable fall distance.
* Powersliding off a ledge at high speeds will not activate Dropdown.

Challenge:
* Dropper

Location:
* North of Apollo.

## Afterboost

Input:
* Dash → Release → Dash

Effect:
* Charge dash until full.
* Release dash.
* While dash bar is red, press dash again.
* Gives velocity boost.
* Activates Afterboost briefly.
* While Afterboost is active, jumping off a ledge causes Long Jump.
* Grants orange bonus momentum until touching ground.

Challenge:
* A Longer Jump

Location:
* South of The Art of Powerslide starting point.

## Wall Boost

Input:
* Air Dash → Ascend x2

Effect:
* Hold Dash midair for Air Dash.
* Look upward and double-tap Ascend against wall.
* Grants significant upward velocity.

Challenge:
* Wall Climb Boost

Location:
* Southwest from Advancement Beta.

## Wall Kick

Input:
* Air Dash → Ascend with back against wall/object while midair

Effect:
* Kicks off wall.
* Redirects player toward direction they are looking.

Challenge:
* Leg Day

Location:
* Roof of Nimbus.

## Chain

Input:
* Ascend after Long Jump conditions

Effect:
* Long Jump begins a chain.
* Landing and quickly jumping off another ledge continues Long Jump chain.
* Speed and distance increase with each chain jump.
* Powerslide and Dropdown preserve chain.

Location:
* Top of Vertigo.
