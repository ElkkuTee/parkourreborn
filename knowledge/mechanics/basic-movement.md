## High Jump

Input:
* Hold Ascend

Effect:
* Holding jump lowers gravity and increases jump height.

Important:
* Holding high jump while moving upward prevents extended coyote / grounded-state trigger.

## Vault

Input:
* Ascend

Effect:
* Smoothly pass over object.
* Hold jump for powerful vault jump.
* Release movement input while holding space to springboard.

## Mantle

Input:
* Ascend

Effect:
* Pulls onto ledge when head is over edge.
* Can allow springboard.
* Faster than normal ledge climb.

## Springboard

Input:
* Ascend while releasing movement keys during mantle/vault

Effect:
* Powerful vertical jump.

## Wall Climb

Input:
* Ascend

Effect:
* Vertical wall traversal.
* Height depends on momentum.
* Press Descend to stop.
* Reticle turns gray when falling too fast to climb.

## Wall Run

Input:
* Forward/backward + Ascend

Effect:
* Horizontal wall movement.
* Speed depends on momentum.
* Wallrun can usually be done twice, but second wallrun needs different wall angle.
* Tapping Descend cuts wallrun short.

## Wall Jump

Input:
* Ascend from wallrun/wallclimb

Effect:
* Any jump out of wall movement.
* Wallrun jumps are generally more powerful than wallclimb jumps.

## Slide / Coil Slide

Input:
* Descend in slope/low-space/steep-surface/coil-landing conditions

Effect:
* Ordinary physical slide.
* Not the same as Powerslide in tech language.

## Air Dash

Input:
* Dash midair

Effect:
* Purple bar appears above momentum bar.
* Enables wall clutch/boost/kick behavior.

## Climb

Input:
* Forwards/backwards while clutching climbable

Effect:
* Move up or down climbable object.

## Climb Leap

Input:
* Ascend while climbing

Effect:
* Leap upward while climbing.

## Climb Jump

Input:
* Ascend while climbing

Effect:
* Jump off climbable. If facing toward climbable, performs Climb Leap.

## Flail Jump

Input:
* Dash → Ascend at edge

Effect:
* Similar to Long Jump but without afterboost.
* Further than normal jump, not as far as Long Jump.

## Impact

Input:
* Descend before landing

Effect:
* Reduces fall damage.

## Fall Stabilization

Input:
* Descend + Ascend midair while falling

Effect:
* Prevents terminal velocity spinout.
