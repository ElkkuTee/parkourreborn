## Momentum

Running builds momentum, shown by the blue bar.

Momentum affects:

* run speed,
* jump height,
* wallrun speed,
* wallclimb height,
* movement strength.

Turning too sharply reduces momentum slightly.

Leveling increases momentum cap up to level 20.

## Temporary Momentum

Temporary momentum is shown by the red/orange part of the bar.

It can be gained from:

* Dash,
* Afterboost,
* some movement actions.

It is lost when touching ground, except it can be extended briefly if jumping once per chain jump, or when dash ends.

Temporary momentum can exceed level cap but not the hard cap.

Dash adds temporary momentum but stops momentum from building further.

Afterboost doubles dash temporary momentum briefly.

## Velocity

Velocity is separate from momentum.

Velocity means actual movement speed and direction.

Velocity does not directly affect movement mechanic strength the same way momentum does.

Advanced movement often increases or redirects velocity without increasing momentum.

## Terminal Velocity

Terminal velocity is when the player spins out of control and cannot survive fall through rolling or clutching.

Fall stabilization is required to stop spinning and survive long falls onto landing pads or sports mats.

## Bhopping

After landing, the player gets 100 ms of air friction instead of ground friction.

Repeatedly jumping can preserve velocity more easily.

## Attack

Attack is the combat ability keybind.

Default:
* Left Click / M1 on keyboard and mouse.

Currently used for:
* Dropkick
