## Dropkick

Input:
* Coil → Attack while midair

Effect:
* Knocks players back based on velocity.
* Stops upward movement.
* Can redirect slightly forward.
* Can be used while swinging to redirect trajectory.
* Landing while dropkicking briefly immobilizes player.

Obtained:
* Level 5
