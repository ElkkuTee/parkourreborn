## Long Jump

Input:
* Afterboost → Ascend at edge

Effect:
* Powerful horizontal jump.
* Can be combined with High Jump.
* Velocity becomes “committed” past momentum, reducing air control unless movement inputs alter it.

## Wall Clutch

Input:
* Air Dash → Ascend

Effect:
* Suspends player on wall briefly.
* Enables Wall Boost.

## Wall Bounce

Input:
* Ascend / wall interaction

Effect:
* Facing wall, tap Ascend midair without movement keys to bounce backward.
* Flicking can redirect velocity.
* Can also happen by quickly exiting a wallclimb.

## Coyote Time / Extended Coyote Time

Effect:
* Makes player act grounded while airborne briefly.
* Advanced tech often uses extended coyote: if player had grounded state last tick and is moving upward, they can keep grounded behavior.
* Jumping during coyote counts as edge jump, so Afterboost can produce Long Jump.

## Jump Boost

Input:
* Coyote Time → High Jump or Ascend

Effect:
* Jump near edge with upward velocity to compound jump force with existing velocity.

## Ledge Grab

Input:
* Ascend

Effect:
* Grabs ledge and raises body onto it.

## Wide Ledge Grab

Input:
* Ascend

Effect:
* Wider grab animation that raises body onto ledge.

## Barrier Vault

Input:
* Ascend

Effect:
* Vault-like motion over thin objects.
