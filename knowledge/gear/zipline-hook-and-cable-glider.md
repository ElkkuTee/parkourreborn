Zipline Hook and Cable Glider allow the player to ride ziplines.

Zipline Hook can be obtained from Derek for:
* 2,500 Credits
* or 100 Tokens

Cable Glider can be obtained from Petra for:
* 15,000 Credits
* or 200 Tokens

## Zipline Hook
Allows the player to ride down ziplines.

## Cable Glider
Adds motorized control over zipline direction.

Level 1:
* can brake while riding ziplines,
* can ride up ziplines for a limited time.
