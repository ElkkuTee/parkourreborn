Mag Rail is an augment gear on the left arm.

It lets the player bounce off walls, slide up climbable props, and depending on upgrade path, bounce toward drones.

There are enough resources to craft two Mag Rails. One can currently be upgraded toward Launch Rail up to level 2.

## Base Mag Rail
Effects:
* launches user forward in the direction they are looking while wallrunning,
* can slide up climbable props.

## Launch Rail
Focus: stronger launches, wall redirection, more ammo, drone magnetization.

Level 1:
* +5% launch strength,
* can launch outward at 45°,
* can bounce upward and redirect near walls,
* can magnetize toward drones.

Level 2:
* +5% launch strength, 10% total,
* can launch outward at 90°,
* can bounce upward and redirect near walls,
* +1 ammo,
* refresh 1 wallrun on use,
* +70% magnetize range,
* +25% magnetize power.
