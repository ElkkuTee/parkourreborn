The Springhook is a core gear on the right arm.

It enhances ledge grabs by increasing distance and speed, and allows launching from ledge grabs.

The Springhook and Powered Springhook I are obtainable.

There are enough resources to craft two Springhooks. One can be upgraded toward Powered Springhook.

## Base Springhook
Effects:
* +50% ledge grab max fall speed,
* +60% ledge grab distance,
* hold to launch from ledge grabs.

## Powered Springhook
Focus: stronger ledge grab and velocity transfer.

Level 1:
* +10% ledge grab max fall speed, +60% total,
* +30% ledge grab distance, +90% total,
* +85% velocity transfer,
* hold to launch from ledge grabs.

Level 2, unobtainable:
* +20% ledge grab max fall speed, +70% total,
* +30% ledge grab distance, +90% total,
* +95% velocity transfer,
* hold to launch significantly from ledge grabs.
