The Glove is a core gear on the right arm.

It affects wall climbing and wall running.

There are enough resources to craft two Gloves. One can go toward Drifter and one toward Climber, but each Glove can only follow one upgrade path and cannot be changed once crafted.

## Base Glove
Effect:
* +25% wall run duration, but actually +50% in practice
* increased wall climb height

## Climber Glove
Focus: verticality.

Benefits:
* increased wall climbing power,
* increased wall boosting power,
* extra push at peak of climbs,
* additional maximum wall climb ammo.

Level 1:
* increased wall boost power,
* extra push at peak of climb.

Level 2:
* +1 max wall climb.

The second wall climb functions like wallrunning in that it must be done at a different angle from the first one.

## Drifter Glove
Focus: horizontality.

Benefits:
* further increased wallrun duration,
* additional wallrun ammo,
* uncapped fall distance,
* vertical speed conversion into horizontal speed.

Level 1:
* +25% wallrun duration, actually +75% and 125% total,
* +1 max wallrun,
* uncapped downward wallrun distance.

Level 2:
* vertical speed converts into horizontal speed when wallrunning.
