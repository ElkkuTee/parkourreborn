* hudzell added the cheese wedge because he saw somebody standing there and thought it would be funny to place a cheese wedge at that exact spot.
* The Golden Vent is likely a Legacy reference.
* Many high-tier valuables spawn around the Golden Vent.
* The neon red orb is nicknamed “Rorb” by the community.
* The Checkpoint Community graffiti logo used to have a translucent version, later replaced with graffiti of a smiling person.
