### Golden Vent
Located in a hidden area surrounded by blue tarps, accessible through a vent behind a plant decoration on the second floor inside the Dirwik Building toward the southern side.
Has many rare resources around it.
Likely references the Golden Vent from PARKOUR Legacy.

### Ruby Vent
Located on top of the Dirwik Building toward the southern side.
Has many rare resources around it, slightly more than the Golden Vent.

### Chrome Vent
Reflects the skybox color.
Located on top of the building directly west from Resources Branch / Celestial, on the same rooftop where Advancement Beta ends.
No resources around it.

### Rusted Vent
Located on a tall building northwest of the Planks checkpoint.
Has many rare resources around it, slightly fewer than the Golden Vent.
