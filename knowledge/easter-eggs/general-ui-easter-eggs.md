### Divided’s Loadout
Rare chance when opening your own or someone else’s loadout.

### “we’ll get ’em next time”
Rare chance when failing a delivery mission.

### “Loneliness - or tranquility?”
Appears under the player’s name in the playerlist when they are alone in a server.
