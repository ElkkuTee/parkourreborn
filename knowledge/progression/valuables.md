Valuables are collectible items around the map that can be sold to vendors for credits.

They are client-sided, meaning other players cannot take them from you.

## Valuable Spawn Types

### Piles
Piles spawn common valuables.

Types:
* Concrete Bricks
* Paper Sheets
* Steel Beams

They respawn every few minutes once looted. If the player is near them, respawning is disabled until the player leaves.

Piles are identifiable by red accent coloring.

### Spots
Uncommon and rare valuables spawn at specific spots around the city, often near hangout places, secluded areas, or points of interest.

The Golden Vent area is an example of a valuable-rich spot.
