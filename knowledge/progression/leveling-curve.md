Total XP for a level:

**Total XP for Level = (Level × 0.8) + ((Level^0.975) / 20)^5**

XP needed between two levels:

**XP Needed = Total XP of Target Level - Total XP of Starting Level**

Current level percentage is a linear measurement of the XP gap for the current level.
