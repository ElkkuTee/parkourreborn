There are two main currencies:
* Credits
* Tokens

Credits are standard in-game currency.

Tokens are premium currency, obtained through Robux purchases, gifts, or the 7th day of Daily Rewards.

All items are purchasable with either currency, but gear usually costs much more in Tokens.

Items affected by data resets are refunded automatically.

Executive Runner doubles credits earned.
