1. In alpha 1.7, credits were capped to 5,000,000 once to address previous exploitative credit methods. This is not a permanent cap.
2. Executive Runner used to double daily token rewards by accident; this was fixed in alpha 1.7.
3. Smartphone used to be Uncommon, then changed to Rare.
4. Before alpha 1.7, vendor sell multipliers were different:
   * Derek and Harper paid one third value for Trash and Scrap, rounded up.
   * Petra paid one half for Scrap, rounded up.
   * Connor was the most profitable because he bought every valuable at full value.
