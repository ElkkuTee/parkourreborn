Daily Rewards can be claimed every 24 hours.

Credits are earned for the first 6 days, and Tokens are earned on day 7.

Rewards increase after the first week.

Executive Runner doubles credits from daily rewards.

## Week 1

| Day | Reward |
| ----: | ----: |
| 1 | 1,000 Credits |
| 2 | 1,000 Credits |
| 3 | 1,000 Credits |
| 4 | 1,000 Credits |
| 5 | 1,000 Credits |
| 6 | 1,000 Credits |
| 7 | 25 Tokens |
