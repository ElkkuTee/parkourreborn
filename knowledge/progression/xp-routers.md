XP Routers permanently increase XP multiplier.

They look like small standing satellite dishes next to laptops.

Router bonuses vary by location:

* Easy routers may grant +5%.
* Tall tower routers may grant +50%.

Maximum multiplier from all routers:
* x12.40

XP Routers affect:
* Combo XP

XP Routers do not affect:
* Race rewards
* Mission rewards

Router trivia:
* In alpha 1.3, a checkpoint and XP Router floated above the ground. The checkpoint could not be synced, but the router could be activated through glitches and gave +5%.
* In alpha 1.5, the intended building was added and the floating router was properly placed.
* In alpha 1.7, two Stack XP Routers were accidentally linked due to sharing a name, making the second bonus inaccessible and lowering max multiplier to x12.10.
* In alpha 1.72, they were unlinked and the intended x12.40 became obtainable.
