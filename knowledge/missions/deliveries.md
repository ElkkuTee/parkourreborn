Delivery missions require the player to pick up a Parcel and deliver it to a specified location within a time limit.

Delivery behavior:

* Requires Runner Phone.
* Julian texts the player when a delivery is available.
* Delivery notifications appear in the bottom right.
* A new delivery appears every few minutes, up to 20 minutes.
* If a delivery is failed, the next one appears 4x faster, around 2 minutes.
* Running around the map causes delivery spawns to appear faster.
* Pickup is shown with a bag icon.
* Dropoff is shown with a flag icon.
* Icons appear on compass and screen markers.
* Parcel spawn spots are fixed, not random.
* Parcel choice depends on the player’s vicinity when Julian sends the notification.

Fail conditions:

* Player dies.
* Player fails to deliver within time.

Failure causes:

* no credit reward,
* fail message shows lost credit amount,
* next delivery comes faster.

Reward behavior:

* Successful deliveries pay credits.
* Longer/farther deliveries pay more.
* District and delivery speed do not affect payout; distance/duration does.
