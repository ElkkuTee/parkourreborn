## Quickbelt
Likely Utility gear.
Expected function:
* bind consumables to keys/buttons for quick use,
* holds a certain amount of consumables,
* upgrades likely increase slots.
Without Quickbelt, consumables only work while stationary and grounded.

## Glaive
Unreleased / vaulted augment gear.
Expected to be used for combat and movement.
Potential movement option:
* pole vault.
Possible upgrade paths:
* Enforcer Glaive
* Drifter Glaive

## Paraglider
Unreleased compound gear.
Returning from PARKOUR Legacy.
Expected function:
* float in air longer.

## Arc Lantern
Unreleased utility.
Expected function:
* illuminates surrounding area with green light,
* especially useful for Asylum,
* may have more uses than light.

## Air Bag
Unknown type.
Expected function:
* last-resort fall damage reducer,
* like portable pad.

## Boost Belt
Mentioned/leaked many years ago.
Possible paths:
* Dash Belt
* Boost Belt
Important:
* Boost Belt is not Quickbelt.
* It may be scrapped.
