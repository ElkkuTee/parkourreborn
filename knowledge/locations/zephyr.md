Zephyr is Fragment’s challenge tower in the Highrise sector.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Tower
* Location: North side of Fragment
* Spawn region: Yes
* District: Fragment
* Currently the tallest tower in the game
* Primarily white cement and blue glass
* Blue/green color scheme
* Name shown in English and Greek letters: ζεΦυρ
* Interior contains modern design, tables, chairs, plants, lighting, and waterfalls
* Players must climb some exterior sections to reach the main entrance
* Longest tower climb in the game
* Elevators take players through different levels
* Final section leads outside to the roof and top transceiver
* Previously referred to as Highrise before alpha 1.5 clarified the tower name
