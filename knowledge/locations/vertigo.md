Vertigo is the tallest tower in Downtown and one of the tallest towers in the map.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Tower
* Location: Northeast from the Tutorial
* Spawn region: Yes
* District: Downtown
* White concrete and blue gridded window pattern
* Entrance is on one of the support legs to the north
* Interior contains rooms that lead to a lounge
* Elevator can be taken to a balcony near the top
* Final climb is mostly exterior
* Shares its name with a tower from PARKOUR Legacy
* Removed in pre-alpha 1.4 for remodeling
* Returned in alpha 1.0 unfinished
* Rework finished in alpha 1.1
* Ambience dynamically changes when entering/exiting as of alpha 1.2
* Exterior logo has changed multiple times
* Balcony area updated in alpha 1.52
