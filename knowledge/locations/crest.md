Crest is a large beige tower in Fragment covered in billboards and connected by many ziplines.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Tower
* Location: Northern side of Fragment, below Highrise
* Spawn region: Yes
* District: Fragment
* Name started as community-created due to similarity to PARKOUR Legacy’s Crest
* Officialized when a checkpoint named Crest was added to the top in alpha 1.7
* A pigeon sits on top of Crest’s transceiver
* Before alpha 1.31, Crest was only fully scalable through advanced movement techniques
* EMAG Accelerator resource is in the boxes interior room
