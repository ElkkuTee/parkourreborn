Apollo is a tower at the southwest edge of Downtown.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Tower
* Location: Southwest edge of Downtown
* Spawn region: Yes
* District: Downtown
* Made of cement texture and blue grid-like window pattern
* Interior accessible through front doors or by breaking windows
* Pipes distributed around the tower allow climbing
* Interior has about 4 floors with furniture, cubicles, chairs, tables, and vending machines
* Reworked in alpha 1.5 and received an interior
* Used to have the Apollo time trial through its interior
* Apollo time trial was added in pre-alpha 1.2 and removed in alpha 1.1
* Apollo and Artemis are named from Greek mythology
* Apollo has a blue screen TV with a dog on it
* Phoebus graffiti is related to the full name “Phoebus Apollo”
