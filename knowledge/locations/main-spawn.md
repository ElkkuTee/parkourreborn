Main Spawn is a building/checkpoint in Downtown and serves as the primary spawn point of the map.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Building
* Location: East from the Tutorial
* District: Downtown
* Glass time trial is on the roof
* A red door leads to a small interior underneath
* “Return to Spawn” in the settings menu sends the player here
* Used to be a physical Roblox spawn plate made of neon-white material with black accents
* Adjacent building’s red glass-cement walls reference PARKOUR Legacy spawn sites
* One Magnetic Coil can be found in the interior
