Artemis is the tallest tower in Dirwik and one of the tallest in the map.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Tower
* Location: South side of Dirwik
* Spawn region: Yes
* District: Dirwik
* Second tallest accessible tower in the game
* Name was initially community-created and later recognized officially
* Artemis is Apollo’s twin sister in Greek mythology
* Entrance is near The Highpoint café
* Players must climb surrounding exterior area before entering
* Contains long vertical passageways, corridors, and rooms
* Final floor is the CEO lounge
* Winch Mount resource is on the round table in the CEO lounge
* Climbing past the CEO lounge leads to roof and respawn transceiver
* Has a one-way fire exit door after descent 1
* Matti easter egg in Security Management Ward is a reanimated YouTube video reference
