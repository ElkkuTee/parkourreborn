PRK Tech, or PARKOUR Technology, is a large building in Dirwik.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Building
* Location: Northeast from Dirwik’s crane, near Petra
* District: Dirwik
* Multiple interior floors
* Logo appears on east and west sides
* Furniture, plants, elevators, cubicles, and balconies inside
* Operational elevator connects second and third floor
* One Nanotube Cable can be found in a cubicle on the second floor
* Second location players travel to during Derek’s questline
* Used to house Grappler Mechanisms before resource changes
