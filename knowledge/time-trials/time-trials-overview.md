Time trials require the player to reach the end goal within a time limit.

Each time trial awards a medal depending on the clear time:

* Bronze
* Silver
* Gold
* Platinum

Platinum medal times only appear after achieving Gold.

Rank stars are awarded by medal:

* Bronze = 1 Rank Star
* Silver = 2 Rank Stars
* Gold = 3 Rank Stars
* Platinum = 4 Rank Stars

Rank stars are cumulative. A Platinum medal counts as a full rank-up worth of stars because it includes the stars from the lower medals.

Players can teleport to time trials they have interacted with.

There are five difficulty levels:

* Basic
* Moderate
* Tough
* Advanced
* Insane

Time trials are gearless. They are one of the main places where pure gearless movement, exact route execution, and world record routing matter.
