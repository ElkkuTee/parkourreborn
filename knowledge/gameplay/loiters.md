## Loiters

Loiters are objects the player can sit or loiter on/off using Interact.

Examples:
* Tree near Solar time trial
* Railing on top of Vertigo near its respawn transceiver and XP Router
* Railing near PRK Tech along bridges
* Railing on top of Artemis near its respawn transceiver and XP Router
* Furniture such as couches, armchairs, and bar stools
