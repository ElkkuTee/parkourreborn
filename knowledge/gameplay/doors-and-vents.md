## Doors and Vents

Doors and vents are entryways.

They can usually be opened by:
* pressing Dash near them,
* using Context Action,
* Powersliding through them.

Door types:
* Red Door
* Gray Door
* F Door
* S Door

Some doors are locked.

Vents often lead into buildings or appear inside interiors, especially challenge towers.
