## Grapple / Swing Points

Grapple or Swing Points are usually flying white drones with red lights and rotor sounds.

They can be:
1. attached to with Grappler,
2. magnetized with Mag Rail,
3. used as swing points,
4. used as launch targets.

Some are still placeholder neon red cubes.

Stack has special drones with their own design, holding objects in the sky.
