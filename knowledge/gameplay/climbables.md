## Climbables

Climbables can be clutched onto by pressing Ascend near them.

Types:
* Pipes
* Ladders
* Window Cleaners
* Elevator Cables
* Poles

Climbables allow the player to climb and can help maintain combo by pausing it while attached.

Mag Rail can let players quickly ascend climbables by pressing Use Augment while climbing.

Poles appear at the top of challenge towers such as Vertigo, Artemis, and Zephyr.
