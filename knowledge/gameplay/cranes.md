## Cranes

Cranes are tall machines used as landmarks.

They vary in height and can be climbed. Sitting in the control room seat lets players change crane direction left/right.

Movable cranes were introduced in alpha 1.7.

They were first thought to be exclusive to Stack but appear operable on all cranes.
