PvP races let players compete against each other by racing to a randomly selected nearby endpoint.

## How PvP Races Work

To invite someone:

* Click the player in the player list.
* Click “Challenge.”
* Invite more players the same way if desired.
* Participants gather around the race creator.
* The host presses START.
* Players race to the endpoint.
* Once the first participant reaches the end, all other participants have 60 seconds to finish.
* After everyone finishes or the 60-second timer runs out, a summary is displayed.
* Players may rematch.

The race finish point is usually determined by whichever endpoint is closest to 150 meters away from the race leader.

PvP races test quick route reading, speedplay decision-making, and consistency under pressure.
