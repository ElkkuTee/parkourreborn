## Dampeners

Dampeners reduce fall damage.

Types:
* Landing Pads
* Sports Mats
* Horizontal Glass

Landing Pads negate damage from any height, but the player must stabilize their fall first when falling from very high up.

Horizontal glass can reduce fall damage when the player breaks through it from above. Press Descend before landing on it, and if needed, again before landing on the ground below.
