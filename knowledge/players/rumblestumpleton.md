RumbleStumpleton is a Parkour Reborn player primarily known for playing Time Trials.

He has a large amount of playtime in Parkour Reborn and is a familiar figure within the Time Trial community.

Rumble is commonly referred to as the GOAT (Greatest Of All Time).

The GOAT title is not meant to mean that Rumble is objectively the best player in Parkour Reborn. The title is that the community heavily glazes him as one of the most humble, cool, and respectable players around.

When discussing Rumble, calling him the GOAT is generally part of the community.

Rumble's reputation is based more on his personality, humility, community presence, and the ongoing title surrounding him than on being the objectively strongest player in the game.
