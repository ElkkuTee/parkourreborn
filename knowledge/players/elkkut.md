ElkkuT is a Parkour Reborn community member and developer known for creating and maintaining the Parkour Reborn Hub website at parkourreborn.com.

He is relatively well-known within the Parkour Reborn community and is involved in various community projects, including development for the Wasans website (wasans.tully.sh).

Known for:

* Creating and maintaining parkourreborn.com
* Developing tools and resources for the Parkour Reborn community
* Contributing for the Wasans website
* Being active within the community
* Running the ElkkuT YouTube channel

ElkkuT does not upload frequently to YouTube, but the channel is associated with his Parkour Reborn presence.
