Cosmetics are decorative and currently mainly consist of skins.

Skins can be applied to gear but do not affect gameplay.

Skins are gear-specific. A Grappler skin and a Glove skin with the same name are separate unlocks.

Skins are obtained through:
* Bags
* Night Market
* Store / premium systems
