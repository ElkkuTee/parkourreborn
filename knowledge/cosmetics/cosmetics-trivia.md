* Skins were introduced in alpha 1.7.
* Skins are the first cosmetic type added to the game.
* Some skins are from or related to PARKOUR Legacy.
* Legacy skin represents how gears looked in Legacy, including Grappler, Glove, and Mag Rail.
* Reborn’s bag system is similar to Legacy, but bags are bought with Credits/Tokens instead of found around the map.
* Reborn skins are gear-specific; Legacy skins could be applied to any compatible gear.
* Basic Blue is specific to Glove because other gears use it by default.
* Worker and Shade bags were introduced in alpha 1.73.
* Alpha 1.73 introduced Ultimate and Mythical rarities.
