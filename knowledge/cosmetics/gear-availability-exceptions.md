Most skins are available for all 6 gear types.

Exceptions:
* Glove: all skins available.
* Grappler: missing Basic Blue.
* Mag Rail: missing Basic Blue.
* Springhook: missing Basic Blue and Legacy.
* Cable Glider: missing Basic Blue and Legacy.
* Zipline Hook: missing Basic Blue, Legacy, Beige Kitten, Tuxedo Kitten, Ahoy!, Black Lotus, White Lotus.
