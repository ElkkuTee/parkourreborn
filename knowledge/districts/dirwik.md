Dirwik is the second district added and was released during the pre-alpha stage.

It is dominated by large buildings, darker colors, and a futuristic atmosphere. At night, Dirwik becomes darker and is lit by neon blue lights.

Important Dirwik facts:
* Type: District
* Location: West of Downtown
* NPCs: 2 total
  * Vendors: 2
* Released November 20, 2023 in pre-alpha 1.4
* Has the most easter eggs of any district
* Currently the only district with its own corporation
* Received environment changes in alpha 1.5
* Initially released unfinished with concrete towers
* One of the two districts not bordering Fragment
* Has a dedicated video on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube channel
