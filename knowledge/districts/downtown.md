Downtown is the first district added to the game and was released with the pre-alpha stage.

It is an urban area focused on the essence of parkour, with a dynamic playground-like environment, many rooftops, office interiors, and references to PARKOUR Legacy.

Downtown is where players first spawn because it contains the Tutorial.

Important Downtown facts:
* Type: District
* Location: Center of Verna
* NPCs: 4 total
  * Vendors: 1
  * Quest NPCs: 1
  * TBD NPCs: 2
* First released district
* Contains the Tutorial and Main Spawn
* Gives 10 Rank Stars when first entered
* Has the most checkpoints of any district: 53
* Borders Fragment to the east, Stack to the north, Dirwik to the west, and Skyarc to the south
* Will likely be the first fully completed district
* First district to have an animated title
* Has a dedicated video on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube channel
