The map of Parkour Reborn is the city of **Verna**.

Verna is divided into 10 districts, with 4 currently released.

## Released Districts

Current released districts:
* Downtown
* Dirwik
* Fragment
* Stack

## Unreleased / Future Districts

Future or unreleased districts:
* Skyarc
* The Mirror
* Axiom
* Citra
* Vertex
* Asylum

## District Positions

1. Downtown: center of Verna
2. Dirwik: west of Downtown
3. Fragment: east of Downtown
4. Stack: north of Downtown
5. Skyarc: south of Downtown, unreleased
6. Fragment borders all but 2 districts: Asylum and Dirwik
7. Dirwik is one of the districts that does not border Fragment
8. Stack borders Downtown and is near Dirwik/Fragment edges in some areas
