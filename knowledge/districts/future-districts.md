## Official Future District Descriptions

| District | Description | Complexity | Danger |
| :---- | :---- | ----: | ----: |
| Skyarc | Upper-class residential highrises densely packed together. Vertically diverse and dangerous. Plants, trees, canopies, and hanging gardens appear throughout the buildings. | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★☆ |
| Axiom | Rich-class district with natural environments inspired by Japanese architecture in a modernized style. Described as the most challenging district due to minimal interconnectivity. Requires creativity and skill to traverse. | ★★★★★ | ★★★★☆ |
| Asylum | Description is blurred. | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ |
| Vertex | A 900-meter tall superstructure, prime construct of the city, monument to human ingenuity, and trade center outside the city. Cannot be climbed or traversed directly from the outside; must be scaled from the inside. | ★★★★★ | ★★★★★ |
