Fragment is the third district added and the first district added during alpha.

It is a modern business district with corporate office buildings, sleek skyscrapers, many explorable interiors, scaffolding, and construction-related props.

Important Fragment facts:
* Type: District
* Location: East of Downtown
* NPCs: 2 total
  * Vendors: 1
  * TBD: 1
* Released August 30, 2024 at 5 PM EST during alpha 1.3
* Has the most interiors of any district: 19
* Highrise sector makes up 20% of the district
* Highrise contains Zephyr, the challenge tower
* Fragment is likely Verna’s center of business, trade, and finance
* Adjacent to all but 2 districts: Asylum and Dirwik
* Has no cranes
* Received animated district title in alpha 1.72
* Has a dedicated video on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube channel

Important Fragment sites and areas:
* Zephyr
* Legacy Foundation
* Crest
* Aven

## Highrise

Highrise is a sector within Fragment, making up about 20% of the district.

It contains Zephyr, Fragment’s challenge tower. Zephyr is the tallest tower in the game.

Highrise is located toward the northern part of Fragment and consists of interconnected modern buildings with white concrete, glass, and blue/green color schemes.
