Stack is the fourth district added and the second one added during alpha.

It is an industrial district with a rustic factory-like atmosphere. It contains construction sites, smokestacks, many props, gray/brown/cement materials, and pink neon lights.

Important Stack facts:
* Type: District
* Location: North of Downtown
* NPCs: 0
* Biggest district in the game as of alpha 1.7
* Has the least checkpoints: 31 as of alpha 1.7
* Most sparsely checkpointed district due to its size
* Has the most cranes: 11
* Stack cranes are uniquely shaped and darker than other district cranes
* Originally planned for beta but released earlier
* Reworked after initial reveal
* Received many renovations in alpha 1.72
* Has two videos dedicated to it on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube channel
