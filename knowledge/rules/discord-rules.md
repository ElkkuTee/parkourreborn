Important Discord rules:
* Users must be 13+.
* Harassment and discrimination are zero-tolerance.
* Hate speech and discriminatory propaganda are forbidden.
* No ragebaiting.
* Use channels for their intended topics.
* No explicit, NSFW, shock, or illegal content.
* Do not spam, flood, ASCII art spam, copypastas, or repost bait.
* Keep conversations topic-relevant.
* Use common sense.
* Advertising is generally forbidden except helpful guide/content sharing where allowed.
* Games infringing on PARKOUR Reborn copyright cannot be shared.
* Mini-modding/backseat modding is discouraged.
* False information about rules/procedures is not allowed.
* Chat filter circumvention is forbidden.
* Misinformation and rumors are not tolerated.
* “The game is dead” / “Parkour Reborn is over” style negative speculation about studio/game vitality is prohibited.
* Speak English outside #international unless no active conversation or no one objects.

Staff and administrators have final say.
