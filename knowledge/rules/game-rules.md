## Exploiting
Using external software, harmful bug abuse, or anything similar to gain advantage or disrupt others is an automatic ban.
Severity affects appealability.

## Bug Abuse
Harmful bug use or repeated non-harmful bug use can result in temporary unappealable ban.
Minimal use of non-harmful bugs may be accepted during Alpha.
Important:
* Techniques like Wumpy, Pogo, and similar movement techs are not bugs.

## Inappropriate Discussion
18+ topics result in temporary unappealable ban.
Excessive taboo/political topics can result in kick or serverban.

## Impersonation
Impersonating another user without permission can result in temporary unappealable ban.
Impersonating to harm someone’s image increases punishment.
Impersonating staff increases punishment heavily.

## Chat Filter Bypass
Bypassing Roblox chat filter can result in temporary unappealable ban.
Extreme cases such as slurs receive stronger punishment.

## Harassment
Harassment can result in immediate serverban or worse.

## Spamming / Flooding
Spam/flooding results in warning, repeated offense can result in serverban.
