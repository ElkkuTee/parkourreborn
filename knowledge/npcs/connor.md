Connor is a Dirwik vendor focused on Springhook.

Location:
* Dirwik
* Inside the Workshop
* Workshop is underground and accessible by elevator or lower door near Celsius time trial
* Connor’s decal marks the garage door

Minimum rank:
* 6

Sells:
* Springhook Chassis
* Tension Compressor
* Steel Extension Spring

Connor’s role:
* Guides player on how to craft Springhook.
* Explains missing parts such as Hook Latch and Frame Extension Unit.
* Marks resource spots with his decal.
* Has the longest dialogue in the game.

Connor arrived in alpha 1.52.

Connor’s Workshop existed before him and was more empty before being used for the Springhook progression.
