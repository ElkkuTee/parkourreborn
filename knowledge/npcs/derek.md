Derek is the second NPC most players encounter after Julian.

Location:
* Downtown
* Around the Solar time trial building
* Can appear in 5 nearby spots
* Derek’s Workshop is across from Solar through a gray door

Type:
* Vendor

Sells:
* Fittings
* Duct Tape
* Rugged Synthetics
* V.D.W. Tape
* Kinetic Fabric
* Runner Phone
* Zipline Hook

Derek’s major importance:
* Sells the Runner Phone needed for story progression.
* Provides a questline at rank 5.
* Rewards Binoculars after the questline.
* Helps players learn the city through exploration tasks.

Derek’s questline locations:
* Legacy Foundation
* PRK Tech
* Apollo Tower
* Crystal
* The Highpoint
* GL Tower hidden dropoff
* Vertigo Tower

Final reward:
* Binoculars
