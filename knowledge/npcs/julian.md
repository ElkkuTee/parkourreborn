Julian is likely the first NPC players encounter.

Location:
* Downtown
* Under a tree on the building players land on after finishing the Tutorial
* New Beginnings checkpoint

Type:
* Quest NPC

Julian introduces the player to the runner network.

Important rewards and progression:
* Gives 500 Credits when accepting the offer.
* Sends the player to Derek.
* Explains hacked phones used for communication.
* After Find Derek / Runner Phone progression, gives the player a rank card.
* Explains deliveries and how courier jobs work.

Julian’s early role:
1. Player finishes Tutorial.
2. Player meets Julian.
3. Julian may require level 3 depending on progression state.
4. Julian gives 500 Credits.
5. Julian directs player to Derek near Solar.
6. Player buys Runner Phone from Derek.
7. Julian gives rank card.
8. Deliveries become part of progression.
