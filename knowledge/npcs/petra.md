Petra is a Dirwik vendor.

Location:
* Dirwik
* Directly across the Dirwik crane, near PRK Tech

Minimum rank:
* 3

Sells:
* Override Module
* Winch Mount
* Nanotube Cable
* Magnetized Alloy Hook
* Micro Pulse Driver
* Fittings
* Cable Glider

Petra is associated with Julian and requires the player’s rank card.

If the player is rank 10 or higher, Petra has different dialogue and treats the player as higher rank than herself.

Petra’s outfit is based on one of the winning entries from the NPC Outfit Contest hosted in the Discord server in May 2024.

Petra is a baddie.
