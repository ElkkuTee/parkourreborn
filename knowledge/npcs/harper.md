Harper is Fragment’s vendor.

Location:
* Fragment
* Aven
* Ascension time trial building
* Has two spawn spots on the building

Minimum rank:
* 8

Sells:
* Bounding Fluctuator
* Aluminum Casing
* Pulse Generator
* Rail Trigger
* Gauss Amplifier
* Magnetic Coil

Harper sells parts necessary for crafting Mag Rail.

If the player is rank 20 or higher, Harper has special dialogue referring to the player as a “bigwig.”

Harper arrived in alpha 1.5 and is the first NPC introduced in Fragment.
